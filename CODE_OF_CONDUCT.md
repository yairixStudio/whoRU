# Code of Conduct

whoRU is a small project and we would like to keep it a pleasant place to work.

## Expectations

- Be kind and direct. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public where possible, so others can learn from them.
- Respect that maintainers and contributors give their time for free. Patience goes both ways.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone else’s private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by opening a confidential GitHub report. Reports are handled discreetly. Consequences range from a warning to removal from the project, at the maintainers’ discretion.

This document is adapted, in spirit, from the Contributor Covenant.
