whoRU sits next to macOS permission dialogs and tells you who is asking, from evidence you can check.

This installs whoRU into your Applications folder and the whoru command-line tool into /usr/local/bin. It takes a few seconds and needs no restart.

When it finishes, whoRU opens in the menu bar and walks you through the one permission it needs: Accessibility, used only to read the text of permission dialogs. An AI agent is optional and can be added later in Settings.
